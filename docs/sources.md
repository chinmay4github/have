# Source notes and evidence policy

[Home](../README.md) · [Reported questions](company-questions.md) ·
[Practice bank](practice-questions.md)

**Research cutoff: September 30, 2026.** This date records when material was
reviewed for this collection. It does not mean the interviews occurred on that
date or that the questions are still in use.

## What the labels mean

- **C — Candidate report:** the relevant public report text was reviewed.
  This is self-reported evidence, not an employer-confirmed account.
- **I — Indexed candidate report:** a search result exposes candidate-reported
  questions, but the full source returned an access-block page. Only the visible
  excerpt supports the entry; surrounding context and dates may be missing.
- **S — Secondary compilation:** an article attributes a question to a company.
  We did not review the underlying individual report for that entry.
- **P — Practice:** an original exercise, without a company attribution.

The reported list contains **30 C entries, 19 I entries, and 3 S entries**.
Counts represent question entries, not independent interviews, unique
candidates, question frequency, or corroboration.

## Editorial rules

1. Preserve the actual role. SWE, MLE, research, agent engineering, and AI
   engineering titles are not interchangeable.
2. Paraphrase questions rather than copying entire articles or answer banks.
   Link to the original publisher for the full context.
3. Separate reported prompts from our preparation pointers. Do not invent
   company-specific follow-ups, constraints, or scoring rubrics.
4. Do not turn a publisher's verification badge into a claim of employer
   confirmation. No interviews in this guide were independently confirmed by
   the companies.
5. Keep publication dates, page-update dates, interview dates, and research
   dates separate. Leave unknown fields unknown.
6. Do not claim that every company asks these questions, that they are the
   latest live questions, or that one report establishes a recurring pattern.
7. Respect access restrictions. Do not bypass logins, paywalls, or security
   blocks to expand an excerpt.

## Date and citation caveats

Some search-result titles use “2026” while the fetched candidate-page titles
use “2025”; several pages only display relative ages. We do not infer an exact
interview date from either a marketing year or a rounded relative timestamp.
Ask the recruiter for current process details.

Citation numbers identify retrieved sources, not evidence strength or a
ranking. Numbers may repeat for different URLs; the URL and publisher identify
the actual source. Republishing the same article on another website does not
create independent corroboration.

## Updating an entry

When reviewing a source again, record the actual review date and access
outcome. A successful fetch is not proof that every field is present or that the
company still uses the question. If only an excerpt remains, downgrade C to I
where appropriate; if no usable evidence remains, flag the attribution for
review or move an independently rewritten exercise to the practice bank.
